Shoubaneh Hemmati

Astrophysicist at Caltech/IPAC

I work on cosmological galaxy surveys and on machine learning for astronomical data. At IPAC I support the Euclid mission through the Euclid NASA Science Center, and my research applies deep learning to survey imaging: deblending, super-resolution, generative models, and self-supervised foundation models spanning Euclid, Rubin, and Roman.

Research

  • AGN variability and changing-look AGN. Data-driven, low-dimensional representations of multiwavelength light curves from ZTF, Pan-STARRS, Gaia, and WISE/NEOWISE that organize AGN classes, separate turn-on from turn-off changing-look AGN, and track spectroscopic and host-galaxy properties. Latest paper: Reducing the Dimensions of AGN Light-curve Manifolds (Hemmati et al. 2026, ApJ 998, 130) · CLAGN Night Sheet, an interactive companion page.
  • Cosmological surveys. Euclid, with contributions to Roman and SPHEREx; survey data processing and science support at IPAC.
  • Machine learning for astronomical imaging. Deblending, super-resolution, diffusion models, and self-supervised foundation models across Euclid, Rubin, and Roman.
  • Manifold learning and dimensionality reduction. Self-organizing maps and related methods for galaxy populations, scaling relations, and SED fitting.
  • Galaxy formation and evolution from multi-wavelength survey data.
